Walk With Me
by paifadmin | Jul 21, 2020 | Projects , Upcoming projects | 0 comments
Walk with me is a free mentorship platform with an opportunity to network with professionals in various industries. We give you the opportunity to mentor or be mentored in any field possible so far as we have the faculty to support.
With over 50 years of experiences gathered from numerous professional and individuals in the various industries not limited to health, education, managerial skills and public speaking, we believe we can lead you to a satisfactory destination.
Feel free and reach out to us on your needs for mentorship or the urge to be a mentor. It will be a great pleasure to have u on board.
Email us at wwm@profferaid.org for any details on Walk With Me.

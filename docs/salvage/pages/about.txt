Our Story
PAIF is an international non-governmental organization. It was established by a group of professionals. The founder, Kofi Bonsu is a Ghanaian based in Italy, formed the organization with the view of reaching the many people situated in the rural-areas of sub-Sahara African countries with the needed health care services. The organization was formed with the support of his other colleague professionals to race in delivering vital health care to remote communities across Africa.

What We Do
The PAIF is working towards bridging the gap between health care services and the patients. As a known fact in Sub-Sahara Africa. People who for a number of reasons are unable to access health care. PAIF works hard to reach such persons who appear to be invisible and voiceless. PAIF organizes health events in deep rural areas where basic health care services are acutely deficient, in-efficient or unavailable. Our main targets are mainly women, children and aged. However generally our programs are organized on extensive basis to reach all persons needing health care.
